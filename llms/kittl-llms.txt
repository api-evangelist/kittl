# Kittl - LLMs.txt

## Platform Overview

Kittl is a browser-based graphic design platform focused on commerce-driven creative workflows. Unlike traditional design tools primarily optimized for social media content creation, Kittl is designed as a complete visual operating system for product-based businesses, independent founders, creators, print-on-demand sellers, agencies, and small commerce teams.

The platform enables users to create professional-quality branding, packaging, product imagery, marketing campaigns, merchandise, labels, mockups, and digital assets without requiring advanced design expertise or external creative agencies.

Kittl's positioning can be summarized as:

"Canva is for content. Kittl is for commerce."

Kittl primarily serves:
- Small business owners (SBOs)
- Shopify sellers
- Etsy sellers
- Print-on-demand businesses
- Direct-to-consumer (DTC) brands
- Freelancers and designers
- Small creative teams
- Agencies managing multiple client brands

The platform emphasizes:
- Fast commercial asset production
- High aesthetic quality
- Brand consistency
- AI-assisted creative workflows
- End-to-end visual commerce operations
